## FAQ

### Can I change plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

### What happens if I exceed my event limit?

We'll notify you at 80% and 100% of your limit. You can:
- Upgrade your plan
- Purchase additional events ($0.50 per million)
- Wait for your limit to reset (monthly)

### Do you offer discounts?

Yes! Discounts available for:
- **Annual billing**: 2 months free (17% off)
- **Nonprofits**: 50% off
- **Academic**: Free for research and teaching
- **Startups**: 50% off first year (under $1M funding)

### Is my data secure?

Yes. PM4Py is SOC2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. See [Security](security.md) for details.

### Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export all your data at any time in XES, CSV, or JSON format.
